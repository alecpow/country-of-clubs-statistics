Country of Clubs Statistics — All Country of Clubs regions

Database snapshot: August 28, 2026

Directory and publishing overview
- The current aggregate includes 3,840 active, non-duplicate Club records for All Country of Clubs regions.
- Those records span 53 state or regional directories.
- The canonical database contains 4,532 Club records; 3,897 have generated pages and 3,020 currently satisfy the search-indexing requirements.

Access-type distribution
- Access type is documented for 3,838 Club records in this scope.
- Private membership: 72.6% (2,788 of 3,838 researched records).
- Other documented access: 10.2% (392 of 3,838 researched records).
- Residential or property-owner: 7.3% (282 of 3,838 researched records).
- Semi-private: 5.1% (197 of 3,838 researched records).
- Public or municipal: 3.3% (128 of 3,838 researched records).
- Resort: 1.3% (51 of 3,838 researched records).

Membership-price coverage
- 2,089 of 3,840 active records (54.4%) contain at least one comparable membership price that meets the benchmark rules.
- 1,853 of 3,840 active records (48.3%) contain a comparable initiation fee that meets the benchmark rules.
- 1,513 of 3,840 active records (39.4%) contain comparable recurring dues that meet the benchmark rules.

Documented minimum first-year cost

Initiation-fee benchmark
- Among 1,262 comparable individual or standard-tier records for documented initiation fees dated 2024–2026, the median is $25,000. The middle 50% runs from $5,000 to $75,000.

Monthly-dues benchmark
- Among 983 comparable individual or standard-tier records for documented monthly dues dated 2024–2026, the median is $625. The middle 50% runs from $370 to $1,000.

Amenities and facilities
- Multiple golf courses are documented at 10.9% of researched records (419 of 3,840; 100% of the active scope has a researched value).

Golf-course scale
- Across 3,840 records with a known course count, Country of Clubs documents 4,457 golf courses.
- The median researched Club has 1 course, and 10.9% (419 of 3,840) document more than one course.

Founding-year context
- Among 1,584 records with a usable founding year, the median documented founding year is 1925. The middle half of the researched sample was founded between 1912 and 1967.

Methodology note
- Every percentage uses the researched sample stated beside it; an empty or unresearched field is never counted as “no.”
- Price benchmarks use recent, compatible records with the same fee type, currency, billing period, and broad membership category. They describe the Country of Clubs evidence set, not every Club in the market.
- Club-level records, individual source URLs, and unpublished values are intentionally excluded from this aggregate report.
