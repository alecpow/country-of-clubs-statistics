# Methodology

## Snapshot and scope

The statistics in this release were generated from the Country of Clubs database snapshot dated 2026-08-28. The principal active scope contains 3,840 active, non-duplicate club records across 53 state or regional directories.

The canonical database and publishing counts are reported separately because they include records outside the principal active aggregate. At the snapshot date, the canonical database contained 4,532 club records, 3,897 generated pages, and 3,020 pages satisfying the current search-indexing requirements.

## Researched denominators

Percentages use the researched sample stated in the corresponding row. Missing, blank, or unresearched fields are never counted as “no.” Consequently, denominators may differ between metrics.

## Access types

Access-type percentages use the 3,838 active-scope records for which an access type was documented. Categories are mutually presented in this aggregate as private membership, other documented access, residential or property-owner, semi-private, public or municipal, and resort.

## Membership-price coverage

Coverage measures whether a record contains at least one comparable membership price accepted by the benchmark rules. Comparable initiation fees and comparable recurring dues are also reported separately.

## Price benchmarks

The first-year cost, initiation-fee, and monthly-dues benchmarks use recent records dated 2024–2026 that are compatible by:

- fee type;
- currency;
- billing period; and
- broad membership category.

The reported benchmark cohort is the compatible individual or standard tier. Medians describe the middle observation, while the lower and upper quartiles describe the middle 50% of the sample. These figures characterize the Country of Clubs evidence set and should not be presented as estimates for every club in the market.

## Golf-course scale

Course totals and the multiple-course percentage use records with a researched course count. For this snapshot, all 3,840 records in the active scope have a researched course-count value.

## Founding-year context

Founding-year statistics use 1,584 records with a usable documented founding year. The dates describe the directory evidence and are not a complete historical census.

## Privacy and research protection

This package intentionally excludes club-level rows, individual source URLs, unpublished values, and the underlying research evidence. It is an aggregate research release designed to communicate reproducible findings without publishing the private database.

## Recommended citation language

When quoting a statistic, include the value, sample size or denominator, scope, and snapshot date. Example:

> Country of Clubs reported a median documented initiation fee of $25,000 among 1,262 compatible individual or standard-tier records dated 2024–2026, based on its August 28, 2026 database snapshot.
