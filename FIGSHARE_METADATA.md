## Description

This aggregate statistical release was produced using the proprietary research database, administrative statistics engine and comparison methodology developed for Country of Clubs. It summarizes 3,840 active, non-duplicate golf and country club records across 53 Country of Clubs state or regional directories as of August 28, 2026.

The release includes directory and publishing coverage, access-type distribution, membership-price coverage, compatible initiation-fee, monthly-dues and documented minimum first-year-cost benchmarks, golf-course scale, and founding-year context. Club-level records, individual source URLs, unpublished values and the underlying research database are intentionally excluded.

**Data provenance:** Calculated by Country of Clubs from its internally maintained structured country club research database using the Country of Clubs statistics and benchmarking tools. Database snapshot: August 28, 2026.
